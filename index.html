Our deep-clean checklist

No area is overlooked. Choose the standard deep clean or go top to bottom for the most detailed result.

Kitchen

  • Stovetop, oven facings, under burners, fan and hood area
  • Fridge facings including handle, top and visible sides
  • Microwave inside, top, sides, underneath and behind
  • Countertops and backsplashes
  • Cupboard doors and dishwasher facings
  • Sink, faucet, drain and drain plug

Bathroom

  • Grout, tubs, tiles and shower enclosure
  • Mirrors
  • Countertops, sink and fixtures
  • Front, sides and behind the toilet, incl. tank, seat and bowl

All rooms

  • Cobwebs removed
  • Closet doors, mirrors and tracks
  • Spot clean doors, frames, window ledges and panes
  • Dust light fixtures, switches, vents and ceiling fans
  • Dust picture frames, decor and all sides of furniture
  • Detailed vacuuming of couches and chairs

Floors

  • Floors thoroughly vacuumed and mopped
  • Carpets vacuumed, including under smaller rugs
  • Hand-washed baseboards
  • Stairs vacuumed and hand-washed
  • Inside empty closets

Kitchen

  • Inside of fridge
  • Sides of fridge and oven (pulled out)
  • Inside of oven, including oven grates*
  • Inside and top of empty cupboards and drawers

Bathroom

  • Wastebaskets emptied, washed and relined
  • Cups, toothbrush and soap holders
  • Inside and top of cupboards and drawers*

All rooms

  • Tops and sides of laundry appliances
  • Mouldings, woodwork, sills and ledges hand-wiped
  • Dust top of and behind books
  • Garbage cans emptied, washed and relined
  • Window interiors and exteriors*
  • Spot-wash walls (latex-painted only)*

Floors

  • Floor surrounding laundry appliances
  • Under area rugs
  • Behind large furniture*
  • Behind large appliances*

* Where safely possible and weather permitting.
