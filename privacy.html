Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Prime Cleaning Team ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Who we are

Prime Cleaning Team is a cleaning services business based in Melbourne, Victoria.
Email: info@primecleaningteam.com.au · Phone: 0452 226 386

2. What personal information we collect

When you contact us or request a quote, we may collect:

  • your name and, if you provide it, your company or organisation;
  • your phone number and email address;
  • your suburb and details about the premises you want cleaned;
  • the service you are interested in and anything you write in your message;
  • records of our communications with you (phone, WhatsApp and email).

Our website hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the website working. We do not collect sensitive information, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you:

  • fill in the enquiry form on this website;
  • message us on WhatsApp, call us or email us;
  • meet us for an onsite quotation or use our services.

When you submit our website form, a copy is emailed to our team and WhatsApp opens on your device with your enquiry pre-filled. The WhatsApp message is only sent if you choose to press "Send".

4. Why we collect it

We use your personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote;
  • arrange, provide and invoice our cleaning services;
  • contact you about your booking or our services you have asked about;
  • improve our services and meet our legal obligations.

If you do not provide your name and a phone number, we may not be able to respond to your enquiry.

5. Who we disclose it to

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with:

  • our staff who need it to quote for or deliver your service;
  • service providers that help us run our business, such as our website and email hosting provider and WhatsApp (Meta);
  • government authorities or others where required or authorised by law.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store information on servers outside Australia. In particular, WhatsApp is operated by Meta Platforms, and our website and email hosting provider may store data in other countries such as Canada or the United States. We take reasonable steps to use providers that protect personal information appropriately.

7. Marketing

We will only send you marketing messages if you have agreed to receive them. Every marketing email or SMS will identify us and include a simple way to unsubscribe, as required by the Spam Act 2003. You can also ask us to stop at any time.

8. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other websites. The fonts and images are served from our own website. If you follow a link to WhatsApp, Meta's own privacy policy applies on its pages and apps.

9. Automated decisions

We do not use automated systems or artificial intelligence to make decisions about you.

10. How we keep it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, and then securely delete or de-identify it.

11. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@primecleaningteam.com.au with the subject "Privacy request". We will respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep some records where the law requires us to.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first at the email above and we will try to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
